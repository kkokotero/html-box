# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want HTML Box to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view. We are committed to making participation respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus review comments on the work.
- Respect different experiences, communication styles, and levels of knowledge.
- Give credit for others' contributions and use project resources responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Publishing someone's private information without permission, or disrupting project spaces, is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior is harmful. To report a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Please include links or context where possible, and do not post private information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Maintainers will respect the privacy of people who report concerns to the extent they can while addressing the issue.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public project channels, and also when someone is representing the project.
